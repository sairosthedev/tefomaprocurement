1Test objective

Run a complete business-process and role-based test of the full FossilProcure system to confirm:

  • All core procurement and stores workflows work end-to-end.
  • Role permissions are correctly enforced.
  • Supplier onboarding, RFQ, quotation, PO, delivery, invoice, and payment workflows complete without blockers.
  • Audit and notification trails are complete and reviewable.
  • The platform is ready for client handover.
Go / No-Go rule

Handover can proceed only if all Priority 1 tests pass and no unresolved Critical or High defects remain.

2Scope: what must be tested

Priority 1  Must pass

  • Authentication and OTP login for all roles.
  • Admin setup: departments, sites, users, supplier activation readiness.
  • End-user requisition through to HOD approval.
  • Stores issue / forward and inventory impact.
  • Procurement RFQ creation and supplier invitation.
  • Supplier quotation submission.
  • Quote evaluation, selection, and PO creation.
  • PO approval chain (HOD → Finance → COO when threshold is met).
  • Supplier PO acknowledgement.
  • Delivery receipt (GRV) and accept into stock.
  • Invoice submission, finance approval, and payment recording.
  • Notifications and audit-log trace completeness.

Priority 2  Strongly recommended

  • Supplier KYS document workflow and verification-status behaviour.
  • Supplier evaluations and analytics visibility.
  • Store requisitions and stock transfers between sites.
  • Budget page visibility and finance-role access checks.
  • Reports visibility by role.
  • Reject / cancel / waiver branches (negative and exception paths).

5Execution plan (full system)

Phase A  —  Access and security checks

Estimated 15 – 20 minutes  ·  Roles: admin, end user, supplier, finance

  1. Sign in as admin (kudzai@fossilzim.co.zw) and verify the OTP flow.
  2. Confirm the staff login page and the supplier login page are both reachable.
  3. Confirm the password-reset flow opens and accepts a valid email.
  4. Verify role-separation basics:
    • End user cannot access admin pages.
    • Supplier cannot access staff pages.
    • Finance cannot access admin-only maintenance pages unless intended.
Expected outcome
All logins work; no obvious permission leakage.

Phase B  —  Admin baseline setup checks

Estimated 20 – 30 minutes  ·  Role: admin

  1. Admin → Departments: create or update at least one test department.
  2. Admin → Sites: verify Head Office exists and add a second site for transfer testing.
  3. Admin → Staff Team: verify each required role account exists and is active.
  4. Admin → Suppliers: verify at least 3 suppliers are active for RFQ competition tests.
  5. Admin → Audit Logs: verify the admin actions above are recorded.
Expected outcome
Master data is complete and auditable.

Phase C  —  Core end-to-end business flow

Estimated 75 – 120 minutes  ·  Roles: all  ·  Execute one requisition all the way to payment

  1. End user creates a requisition with:
    • one stock-catalog line,
    • one manual / free-text line,
    • realistic quantity and category values.
  2. Department head approves the requisition — and separately rejects a throwaway requisition with a reason.
  3. Stores reviews the requisition:
    • issue the in-stock line(s),
    • forward the non-stock line(s) to procurement.
  4. Procurement creates an RFQ from the forwarded items and invites suppliers.
  5. Supplier submits quotation(s).
  6. Procurement reviews quotations and selects the winner.
    • If fewer than 3 quotes are received, complete the waiver path with a reason.
  7. Procurement creates the PO and submits it for approval.
  8. HOD approves the PO.
  9. Finance approves the PO.
  10. COO approval branch:
    • test one PO below threshold (COO not required),
    • test one PO at or above threshold (COO required).
  11. Supplier acknowledges the PO.
  12. Stores receives goods (GRV) and accepts them into stock.
  13. Supplier submits an invoice linked to the PO.
  14. Finance approves the invoice and records payment.
  15. Admin validates the complete audit and notification trail for the transaction.
Expected outcome
The end-to-end lifecycle completes with correct status transitions at every step.

Phase D  —  Extended module checks

Estimated 30 – 60 minutes  ·  Roles: admin, procurement, stores, finance, supplier

  1. Supplier KYS flow: upload documents · verify checklist progress · confirm activate / verify behaviour.
  2. Supplier evaluation flow: create an evaluation score · verify the score appears in analytics / performance.
  3. Stores extras: test a store requisition · test a stock transfer between two sites.
  4. Finance extras: verify invoice state transitions · verify payments-ledger entry visibility.
  5. Reports and dashboards: verify each role sees the correct report scope.
Expected outcome
Non-core but business-critical modules are stable and role-correct.
