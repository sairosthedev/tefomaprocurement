Part 11

For suppliers

This part is for supplier companies. You register once with each organisation you supply through Procureline, upload your compliance documents, quote online, acknowledge orders, follow deliveries and invoice from the same place. You see only your own records.

Your menu. Bidding (My RFQs, Submitted Quotations), Orders (Purchase Orders, Deliveries, Invoices) and Account (Company Profile, KYS Documents), plus Notifications. Your dashboard shows open RFQs, orders and invoices at a glance.

11.1 Register your company

  1. Open the buyer's Procureline address and click Register your company under the sign-in box. The form has five steps: Account, Company, Address, Banking, Review.
  2. Account. Your name, Email Address, Phone Number and a password of at least 6 characters. The email is your sign-in and receives every code and notice; the phone number is the one Procurement will call to confirm bank details.
  3. Company. Company Name and Registration Number are required. Add trading name, VAT number, business and entity type, and the Product/Service Categories you supply; categories are how you get invited to RFQs.
  4. Address and Banking. Your business address and country, and the bank account you want to be paid into.
  5. Review and click Complete Registration.

The page says Registration Successful! and that your account is pending approval. Sign in (2.2) and go straight to KYS Documents. If the page says This company is already registered, sign in with the existing login or contact the buyer's Procurement team.

11.2 Upload your KYS documents

Open Account › KYS Documents. The banner at the top says where you stand; the list is split into Mandatory documents and Optional documents.

  1. For each mandatory document click Upload and choose the file (PDF, image, Word or Excel, up to 5 MB).
  2. Where the row asks for an Expiry date (tax clearance, NSSA, insurance, ISO, licences, NEC) enter it before uploading; it must be a future date.
  3. Watch the row: awaiting review becomes verified when Procurement has checked it, or Rejected with the reason, in which case Replace it.

When Procurement has everything, it puts your account forward for activation and a manager approves it. The banner then reads Your account is active and verified and you are emailed. Until then you can still be invited to quote, but you cannot be awarded work.

Mandatory for every supplierAlso mandatory for critical, strategic and unclassified suppliers
Company Registration CR14 (list of directors); Tax Clearance CertificateRegistered Address CR6; NSSA Compliance Certificate; Company Profile; Client Referrals (three previous clients); Audited Financial Statements (past three years); Bank Reference; Safety Records; Environmental Policy; Insurance Coverage; Disaster Preparedness Plan

Keep expiring documents current: reminders come 60, 30 and 7 days before expiry, and an expired mandatory document stops new awards until it is replaced

11.3 Your company profile and bank details

Account › Company Profile shows your Supplier Code, your permanent number on the buyer's register, and the status badge of your account (11.8). Company name, registration, tax and VAT numbers can only be changed by Procurement. You can update your contact person, phone, address and Service Categories, and click Save Changes.

Changing bank details. Edit the Bank Details section and save. The change does not take effect at once: the page says it needs verification and approval. Procurement will telephone a number already on your record, then a second person approves. The status line under Bank Details tells you where it is: waiting for the call-back, confirmed by phone, approved, or not approved. Payments to you are held while the change is open, so make the change well before an invoice falls due, and make sure the phone number on your profile is answered.

11.4 Quote on an RFQ

  1. You are emailed New RFQ when a buyer invites you. Open Bidding › My RFQs; the row shows the closing date and time remaining. Click View to read the items, specifications, delivery requirements and terms; any equipment photos the requester attached are shown with the line.
  2. Click Submit Quote. The drawer has three steps: Prices, Terms, Review & submit.
  3. Prices. Choose the currency, then enter a Unit Price for every line and the Brand. If you are offering an equivalent rather than the exact part, tick I am offering an equivalent / alternative part, say what it is and its part number, and attach photos. Lines you cannot supply still need a price to submit; say so in the notes.
  4. Terms. Delivery (Days), Payment Terms (cash, net 7, 14, 30 or 60), Valid For (Days) and any notes.
  5. Review & submit. Check the grand total, then click Submit Quotation. Once submitted, a quotation cannot be edited.

Toast Quotation submitted successfully. Your bid is sealed: nobody at the buyer can see it until the RFQ closes. Follow it under Bidding › Submitted Quotations: Submitted, Under Review, then Accepted or Rejected. If Procurement asks for a price revision, the RFQ shows a banner; submit a new quote, which replaces the old one.

11.5 Acknowledge a purchase order

  1. When an order is fully approved you are emailed the order PDF. Open Orders › Purchase Orders; new orders show Acknowledge.
  2. Click it, add your Delivery Note Number and Expected Delivery Date if known, and click Confirm Acknowledgment.
  3. Open the order for the full detail and Download PDF.

Toast Purchase order acknowledged — next, deliver the goods. The order is now issued, the buyer's Stores expects the delivery, and Orders › Deliveries shows it as pending. Acknowledge promptly: the buyer is told when you do, and an unacknowledged order is one the buyer will chase.

11.6 Deliveries

Deliver with a delivery note quoting the order number. Stores counts and inspects the goods and raises a goods received voucher (GRV). Orders › Deliveries shows each delivery with its GRV number and status: Pending, Received, Accepted, Partially accepted or Rejected, and the quantities received and rejected per line. You are notified of what was accepted and rejected on every GRV. Faulty goods are returned within five days of delivery. Deliver the balance of a short delivery on a new delivery note; it gets its own GRV.

11.7 Submit an invoice and get paid

  1. Open Orders › Invoices and click Submit invoice. Three steps: Order & invoice, Lines & VAT, Review & submit.
  2. Order & invoice. Pick the Purchase order (only issued orders with goods received are offered), enter Your invoice number and the Invoice date.
  3. Lines & VAT. The lines come from the quantities Stores received, not from the order. Charge the same VAT as the order carries, or the invoice will not match.
  4. Review & submit. The page compares the order and your invoice and says whether they match. Click Submit for three-way match.

Toast Invoice submitted successfully. Finance matches it against the order and the GRVs. The status moves from Submitted to Approved and then Paid; Variance means Finance is looking at a difference, and Rejected shows the reason, after which you submit a corrected invoice. Open an invoice to see the Payments table with the date, method and reference of each payment, and the balance due. Payment runs are on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

Part 12

Help

Most problems are one of the following. Your first call is your administrator; a supplier's first call is the buyer's Procurement team. When you report a problem, give the record number (for example REQ-0042 or PO-0017) and what the screen said.

12.1 Common problems

I signed in but no code arrived.
Check spam. The code goes to the address on your user record; if that is wrong, the administrator fixes it under Staff Team. Codes expire after 10 minutes; click Back to sign in to get a new one. If nobody in the organisation receives codes, email sending has stopped: tell the technical team.
The page says my session is refused or sends me back to sign in.
You are at another organisation's address, or your session expired. Use your own organisation's address and sign in again.
I cannot see a button the manual describes.
Buttons appear only for the role that performs the step, and only when the record is at that step. Check the status badge: the record is still with the person before you. If your role is wrong, the administrator changes it.
My requisition has been "Pending HOD Approval" for days.
The department head has not acted. Their Approvals and Team Requisitions pages mark it overdue. If they are away, the administrator names a deputy on the department, who can approve.
I cannot submit my requisition.
It needs a title and at least one item. A new item not in the catalogue is fine; describe it.
Procurement cannot select or authorise my chosen supplier.
The supplier is not activated, its KYS is short for its tier, or a required document has expired. Open the supplier: the facts strip shows Spend authorisation and KYS progress. Complete the file and submit for activation (7.5), or replace the expired document.
The RFQ closed with fewer than three quotes.
A waiver from the COO or Head of Procurement is needed (6.5, 8.3), or re-source with more suppliers invited. Check that invited suppliers can actually sign in; help them register (11.1).
The order went to the COO although it is small.
It is in a currency with no exchange rate on file, so the system routes it to the COO rather than guess. The Procureline operators set the rate (10.4).
Finance cannot approve an invoice.
Either there is no GRV for the goods (Stores must receive them first, 5.3), or the invoice exceeds what was received and not yet invoiced. Reject it so the supplier submits a corrected one.
A supplier says they changed their bank details but payments still go to the old account.
The change is waiting for the call-back or the second approval (7.7). Until it is approved, the old account stands and payments are held.
Stock on the screen does not match the shelf.
A movement was not recorded. Find it in Stock Movements and record the missing issue, receipt or transfer. Do not edit the balance.
No reminders or overdue alerts are arriving.
The hourly jobs are not running on the hosted API. The administrator checks the audit log for the job and tells the technical team (1.2, item 6).
A supplier cannot register: "This company is already registered".
A record with that registration number exists, perhaps created by Procurement. The supplier signs in with that login or uses Forgot password?; otherwise Procurement looks the record up by registration number.

TermMeaning
Activation requestA pending supplier put forward by Procurement for a second person to approve. Open until approved, returned or rejected
AwardThe decision to buy from a supplier's quotation, in full or line by line
Business unitWhat Procureline calls an organisation it serves: its own address, its own database and its own settings
COO limitThe order value in US dollars at or above which the COO must approve. The default is USD 5,000
CR14 / CR6Company registration forms: the list of directors (CR14) and the registered address (CR6)
Deputy headA user who can act for a department head on that department's requisitions
GRVGoods received voucher: the Stores record of what physically arrived
Head of ProcurementThe department head of the Procurement department. Authorises awards and gives the first approval on orders
IRInternal requisition: a requisition as seen by Stores during stock review
KYSKnow Your Supplier: the due diligence checklist a supplier must pass before it can be paid
MSDSMaterial safety data sheet, required for hazardous goods
NSSANational Social Security Authority; its compliance certificate is a KYS item
POPurchase order: the commitment to buy, sent to the supplier after approval
Requisition (PR)A request from a department for goods or services
Rejected (registration)A supplier registration turned down with a reason. The company cannot quote or be awarded; the registration can be reopened. Not a blacklisting
RFQRequest for quotation, sent to invited suppliers
Sealed bidA quotation nobody can see or compare until the RFQ closes
Spend-authorisedA supplier whose activation has been approved and who may receive awards and orders

TierA supplier's risk class (critical, strategic, tactical, transactional, unclassified), which sets the depth of its KYS and how often it is reviewed

WaiverApproval to award with fewer than three quotations, with a reason from a fixed list
