Summary

At a glance

Procureline runs every purchase in your organisation from the request to the supplier's payment, with your procurement rules built in. This manual is for the people who bring it into use: what must be true before anyone buys through it, how to set the organisation up, how to pilot, and how to go live without losing a purchase on the way.

How to read the steps

  • Organisation › Staff Team is a menu path: open Organisation in the left-hand menu, then click Staff Team.
  • Add User is a button or link exactly as labelled on screen.
  • Numbered steps are done in order. The grey line after a procedure says what you should see when it has worked.
  • References such as User Manual 4.3 point to the numbered section of the User Manual.
