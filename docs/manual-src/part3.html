Part 6

Sourcing and orders

Procurement takes a requisition from acceptance to a signed-off order: invite suppliers, collect sealed quotations, pick the winner with a reason, have the award authorised, and raise the order. The system will not let a step be skipped, so the quickest path is the correct one.

6.1 Your dashboard

Open RFQs, Pending Quotations, Active Suppliers and Purchase Orders, plus a Requisition pipeline with tiles for Pending Acceptance, Accepted, In Sourcing and Ordered. Needs attention lists requisitions awaiting acceptance, quotations awaiting evaluation and open RFQs collecting quotations. The Head of Procurement also sees suppliers awaiting their activation approval. Quick actions: Create New RFQ, Review Quotations, Manage Suppliers, Purchase Orders.

6.2 Accept a requisition

Procurement officer
  1. Open Sourcing › Requisitions (titled Purchase Requisitions). Rows at Pending Acceptance have Accept and Reject.
  2. Open it with View if you want to trim it: while it is with you, lines can be edited or removed, with the hint Remove any item you don't want purchased before approving.
  3. Click Accept and confirm. To refuse it, click Reject with a Reason for Rejection; the requester is told.

Toast Requisition accepted — now create its RFQ, and the Create RFQ drawer opens with the requisition's lines already in it. The two-day sourcing clock starts; the RFQ list shows overdue after that.

6.3 Create and publish an RFQ

  1. From the accepted requisition click Create RFQ, or open Sourcing › RFQs and click Create RFQ. The drawer has four steps.
  2. Details. RFQ Title and Submission Deadline are required. Add Description / Requirements, Delivery Required By and Terms & Conditions.
  3. Items. The lines come locked from the requisition and cannot be changed here. If they are wrong, reject the requisition instead.
  4. Suppliers. Suppliers in the requisition's categories are pre-selected. Use Add n related to top up from related categories, or search by name. The drawer warns when fewer than three suppliers are invited and when two records look like the same company. Invite at least three; a closed RFQ with fewer than three quotes needs a waiver (6.5).
  5. Review & send. Check everything; each block has Edit. Click Publish RFQ, or Save as Draft.

Toast RFQ created and sent to suppliers. Every invited supplier gets a notification and an email headed New RFQ, and a reminder in the 48 hours before the deadline. A draft is published later from the RFQ's own page with Publish RFQ.

Until the deadline, bids are sealed: the RFQ page says Bids are sealed, the Quotations list shows Sealed bid instead of the supplier and amount, and nobody in the organisation can open one. This is deliberate.

6.4 Close the RFQ and compare bids

  1. Open the RFQ and click Close RFQ & Reveal Bids. Confirm; suppliers can no longer submit.
  2. Read the Response Ranking panel: each quotation is scored out of 100 (price 50, delivery 20, past performance 20, completeness 10) with tags such as Lowest price and Fastest delivery, the lowest price and the spread. The ranking is advice, not a decision.
  3. Open each quotation with View quotation to read the lines, brands, any Alternative offered note, delivery days, payment terms and validity.

Toast RFQ closed — bids are now visible. The RFQ shows Closed, and the requisition's RFQ card reads Under evaluation.

6.5 Fewer than three quotations

If the RFQ closed with fewer than three quotations, the award is blocked until the COO or the Head of Procurement approves a waiver. The COO and Head of Procurement are notified at closing so it does not sit unnoticed. On the quotation page, the Acceptance Authorization panel's first step, Minimum 3 competitive quotations, offers the waiver; on the award matrix each short line has Waiver. The approver picks a type: Single/sole source, Insufficient quotations received, Existing service agreement, Approved framework / contract, Unique / proprietary product, Custom manufacture / bespoke item, COO directed procurement, COO written instruction, or Other, and clicks Approve waiver. A procurement officer cannot approve their own waiver.

6.6 Select the winner

Procurement officer

There are two ways to award: the whole RFQ to one quotation, or line by line to different suppliers.

Whole quotation

  1. Open Sourcing › Quotations and View the winning quotation.
  2. In Acceptance Authorization, step Procurement selection with justification, write the Justification for selecting this quotation and click Select this quotation.

Toast Quotation selected. Waiting for the Head of Procurement to authorize. The Head of Procurement sees it under Authorizations (8.1). You cannot authorise your own selection.

Line by line (split award)

  1. On the RFQ click Split Award. The matrix shows one row per line and one column per supplier, cheapest first, with the spread per line.
  2. Click the price you are awarding on each line and type the Justification when asked. The line shows Awarded (needs auth).
  3. The Head of Procurement authorises each line with Authorize. Then click Generate POs for authorized lines: one order is created per supplier.
  4. Lines nobody quoted, or that you did not award, go back out with Re-source unquoted / unawarded lines, which creates a new RFQ and notifies its invitees.
The supplier must be ready. An award is refused if the supplier is not activated (7.5), its KYS is incomplete for its tier, or a required document has expired. Check the supplier's facts strip (Spend authorisation: Authorised) before you select.

6.7 Request a price revision

On a quotation that is submitted or under review, Request Revision asks the supplier for a better price with a Reason for revision and optional target. The quotation shows Revision Requested; the supplier's new quote supersedes the old one. Use this before selection, not after.

6.8 Accept and raise the order

  1. Once the panel says Fully authorized — you can now accept this quotation, click Accept and confirm. The other suppliers are told their quotation was not selected.
  2. Click Create Purchase Order on the quotation, or Create PO from Quotation on Sourcing › Purchase Orders. The drawer has four steps: Quotation (pick the accepted quotation), Delivery (address and Expected Delivery Date), Terms and Review. Click Create Purchase Order.
  3. Open the order and click Submit for Approval.

Toast Purchase order submitted to the Head of Procurement for approval. The order shows Pending Head of Procurement. One quotation can have only one order.

To cancel, open the order and click Cancel PO with a reason from the list (supplier unable to deliver, price or terms changed, goods no longer required, duplicate, linked requisition cancelled, supplier default, other). Cancellation is blocked once goods have been received or an invoice is active. The supplier is notified.

6.10 Evaluate the supplier

After delivery, score the supplier under Suppliers › Evaluations. The Due for review tab lists suppliers that are overdue or never evaluated. Click Evaluate: score the seven criteria from 1 to 5 (credit terms, contracts in place, market reputation, pricing, delivery, ease of dealing, consistent quality), choose the Evaluation type and a Recommendation (Approve, Conditional approval, Re-evaluate later, Reject), add notes and Save evaluation. The score feeds the past performance part of future rankings and the Performance view. Re-evaluation falls due every 3, 6, 12 or 24 months by tier.

Part 7

Supplier management

A supplier can be asked for a price while its checks are in progress, because a quote commits nothing. It cannot win an award or receive an order until its Know Your Supplier (KYS) checks are complete, a second person has approved its activation, and it is marked spend-authorised.

7.1 Add a supplier

Procurement officer

Suppliers can register themselves (Part 11), or you can create them. Either way they start as Pending.

  1. Open Suppliers › All Suppliers and click Add Supplier. Four steps: Company & login, Business, Banking, Review.
  2. Company & login. Company Name, Registration Number, contact First Name and Last Name, and Email are required. Leave Password blank to have one generated.
  3. Business. Optional: trading name, phone, tax and VAT numbers, Business Type (manufacturer, distributor, wholesaler, retailer, service provider, contractor, other), Entity Type (company, individual), address, and Categories of Supply. Categories are what match a supplier to RFQs, so set them.
  4. Banking. Optional first bank account. Later changes go through bank-change approval (7.7).
  5. Review and click Create Supplier.

Toast Supplier created — complete KYS to activate them. If a password was generated, the Supplier created box shows it once; it is also emailed to the supplier, who should change it at first sign-in. Continue to KYS opens the profile on the Documents tab. The supplier gets a permanent code such as ORG-SUP-00042 (your organisation's code, then a running number), shown under its name.

A company can be registered once. The registration number is compared ignoring case, spaces and hyphens, so a second record for the same company is refused.

7.2 Bulk import

Bulk Import on the supplier list takes an Excel, CSV or JSON file, or pasted CSV with a header row. Required columns: companyName, registrationNumber, contactPerson, email, phone; categories is optional. The preview highlights cells missing a required value; those rows are rejected and the rest imported. Imported suppliers start as pending and still need KYS and activation. Use this for the supplier register at launch.

7.3 KYS documents and checklist

Open the supplier and the Documents tab. It is a three-step wizard: Documents, Checklist, Activate.

  1. Documents. The list is split into Mandatory documents for the supplier's tier and Optional documents. Suppliers upload their own (11.2); you can also Upload or Replace for them. Each file is PDF, image, Word or Excel, up to 5 MB. Tax clearance, NSSA, insurance, ISO, industry licences and NEC need an Expiry date.
  2. Check each file. Open it with View, then Verify it, or Reject it with a reason the supplier sees. A rejected or expired file shows why.
  3. Checklist. Tick each item as satisfied; the tick saves at once. The counter shows required items done against the tier's requirement. Items are grouped: registration and statutory (CR14, CR6), tax clearance, NSSA and NEC, licences and ISO, company capability (profile, three client referrals), financial standing (audited statements, bank references, payment terms, liquidity), commercial and operations, and health, safety and environment (safety records, environmental policy, insurance, disaster plan).
Expiry matters on its own. A required document that has passed its expiry date blocks awards and orders to that supplier, whether or not anyone has noticed. Reminders go to the supplier and to Procurement 60, 30 and 7 days before.

7.4 Set the diligence tier

On the Activate step, the Diligence tier panel sets how deep the check must be. An unclassified supplier is treated as full depth, so low risk is never assumed. Click the tier and give the reason when asked. The panel suggests a tier from what it knows.

7.5 Submit for activation

Procurement officer or Head of Procurement
  1. On the Activate step, click Submit for activation. The box reminds you that the Head of Procurement or the COO, not you, approves it, and that the supplier stays pending until then.
  2. If KYS is short of the tier's requirement, the box lists what is missing and a Reason for the KYS override is required; the approver sees it and, by approving, takes the override in their own name. Otherwise add Notes for the approver if useful.
  3. Click Submit.

Toast Submitted for activation. The supplier shows Awaiting approval with a banner naming you and the date. Every Head of Procurement and COO other than you is notified. If it comes back returned, the banner shows the reason; close the gap and submit again.

The supplier list filter Awaiting approval shows every open request; approvers also have Awaiting my approval. The approver's steps are in 8.4.

7.6 Suspend, blacklist, reactivate

The buttons sit beside the status badge on the supplier's page. Each asks for a reason and confirmation, and each is written to the audit log and the supplier's status history.

ButtonWhen shownEffect
SuspendActiveNo new invitations, awards or orders until reactivated
Mark dormantActiveStays on the register but set aside until reactivated
ReactivateSuspended or dormantBack to active. Not onboarding, so no second approval
BlacklistAny statusCannot be invited, quote, be awarded or register again; the reason is shown on the record
Lift blacklistingBlacklistedBack to pending. It must be submitted for activation and approved by someone else before it can win work

Rejecting or reopening a registration is for the Head of Procurement or the COO (8.5). Rejection says nothing against the company, unlike blacklisting.

7.7 Bank-detail changes

Procurement officer, two different people

A change to where a supplier is paid is the classic fraud, so it is handled on Suppliers › Banking Changes with a call-back and a second person. Payments to the supplier are held while a change is open.

  1. The supplier changes its bank details on its Company Profile (or you edit the Banking tab for them). The change is held as a request, shown as pending verification with the old and new values side by side.
  2. Call back. Telephone the supplier on a number that was already on file before the request. The numbers on file are frozen while the request is open, so a number supplied with the change can never be the one called. Then click Record callback, enter the number you called and who confirmed the change. Calling a number not on file needs an override reason and is logged.
  3. Approve. A different person, who is neither the requester nor the caller, clicks Approve. Or Reject with a reason.

Toast Banking change approved and applied. Payments have resumed. The supplier is told either way and sees the outcome on its profile.

7.8 Vetting record

On the supplier's Vetting tab, record how the supplier joined the register (Onboarding method: newspaper invitation, direct application, referral, existing supplier, other) and the Site visit: status, date, who conducted it and notes. Click Save vetting record. The Directors and References tabs hold contacts and at least three trade references, which the KYS checklist expects.

7.9 Compliance and performance views

  • Suppliers › Compliance: KYS verified and pending counts, coverage, suppliers pending activation, and a Compliance watchlist with incomplete KYS first. Start here each week.
  • Suppliers › Performance: scores from evaluations, bands from Excellent to Low, top suppliers, and who is Due for evaluation.
  • Suppliers › Evaluations: record and browse evaluations (6.10).
  • Reports, Suppliers tab: the registry with status, KYS, score, orders and spend per supplier, exportable to CSV.
